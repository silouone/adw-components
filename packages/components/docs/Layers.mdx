# Layers

Components sit in five layers, in the order Storybook lists them. The level is decided by actual
composition, not by how big a component looks, and a promotion from one layer to the next is a
folder move with no import-path change.

## Atoms

Composes no other component and carries no domain. `Kpi` is the example: a glyph and a value in a
pill.

## Molecules

Composes atoms, and may carry a domain word. `ArmButton` and `PhaseStrip` are the examples.

## Organisms

A region of a screen with a view-model: a card, a ledger, a rail. It renders from its typed
`<Name>Props` and reports through callbacks.

## Templates

A frame that places organisms by slot: the three-column shell, the focus panel frame, the dock
frame, the card grid section.

## Pages

A template filled with organisms, taking one `<Screen>View` and one `<Screen>Actions` object. Its
story is fed by an invented fixture, never by a real run.
